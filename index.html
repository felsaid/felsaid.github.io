Published · IEEE OJ-ITS, 2026

Comparative analysis of spatio-temporal graph neural networks for passenger flow forecasting

Toronto subway network · TTC

11STGNN architectures
61Subway stations
12 moWi-Fi connection data
>35%MAE cut vs LSTM, peak
  • Benchmarked CNN-, RNN-, attention-, ODE-based and hybrid models against an LSTM baseline for 15 to 60 minute forecasting, using Wi-Fi device connections as a passive proxy for ridership.
  • Dynamic and adaptive graph learning models (D²STGNN, Graph WaveNet) were the most accurate. Graph WaveNet and STGCN were the most efficient. The MAE cut is for morning peak at the most improved stations.

doi:10.1109/OJITS.2026.3741013
